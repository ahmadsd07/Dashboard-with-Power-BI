## Project Overview

This dashboard helps monitor business performance through key sales metrics and enables users to analyze trends across different years, stores, products, and customer segments.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor sales performance from 2023–2025.
- Compare business performance Year-over-Year (YoY).
- Identify top-performing and underperforming stores.
- Analyze product category and sub-category performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel


## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Gross Profit Margin
- Number of Transactions
- Total Customers
- Average Transaction
- Promotion Transaction %
- Return %


## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview
Provides an overall summary of business performance, including Revenue, Gross Profit, Gross Margin, Transactions, and monthly sales trends.

### 2. Store & Product Analysis
Analyzes sales performance by store, region, product category, and sub-category to identify top and low-performing areas.

### 3. Customer Analysis
Analyzes customer demographics, payment methods, customer segments, promotion usage, and transaction behavior.

## Key Insights

- Revenue increased in **2024** before declining in **2025**.
- Gross Profit followed a similar trend, indicating a slowdown in business performance during 2025.
- Gross Profit Margin remained relatively stable, suggesting cost efficiency was maintained despite lower sales.
- Transaction volume slightly decreased in 2025, indicating reduced purchasing activity.
- Dashboard analysis helps identify stores, products, and customer segments that require further business evaluation.

## Business Recommendations

- Investigate stores and products with declining sales performance.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-margin products.
- Evaluate promotional strategies to improve sales effectiveness.
- Strengthen customer retention through loyalty programs.
- Continuously monitor KPIs to support strategic decision-making.
